AI architecture · Decisions and safeguards

Guide the practice.
Keep faculty assessment distinct.

The AI Video Grader work centers on a beta finding: some learners mistook AI practice feedback for a final grade. The design response makes the purpose, authority, and next step of feedback explicit.

Define the feedback boundary

AI guidance supports practice. Faculty assessment remains a separate responsibility. The interface should make the difference visible when learners receive feedback.

Tie feedback to evidence

A proposed technical safeguard is to connect each observation to the approved rubric and the relevant recording segment. If the recording does not support an observation, request review rather than inventing a judgment.

Design for uncertainty and access

A connected build needs permission checks for recordings, clear retention rules, and failure paths for incomplete uploads or unusable audio. These are validation requirements, not verified production controls.

The supplied adaptive tutoring prototype is assigned to this AI Video project for review. Its simulated tutoring experience is not evidence that a video-analysis pipeline or an official grading integration is deployed.
