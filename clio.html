Problem & product boundary

A connected curriculum.
A visible chain of evidence.

Course maps managed across Word and Excel made it difficult to trace a program outcome to weekly teaching, compare versions, or see what a revision would affect. Clio organizes that work around a shared curriculum map and an explicit approval process.

SME editor

Inspect coverage, propose revisions with rationale, and review AI suggestions.

Reviewer / approver

Review changes, record decisions, and prepare evidence. A drafter must not approve their own change.

Dean

Understand program health and readiness through a read-only leadership view.

Accreditation reviewer

Inspect a narrower set of curriculum evidence without edit access.

AI architecture · Decisions and safeguards

Suggest a connection.
Protect the approved curriculum.

Clio is an interactive prototype with an engineering handoff. Its intended architecture uses one model-based framework suggester and eleven fixed workflows. Suggestions never silently change the approved map.

Use AI for interpretation

Compare outcome text with an approved framework and return a reviewable suggestion with confidence and expiry. The editor chooses whether to accept or ignore it.

Use rules for coverage and authority

Calculate coverage and progression from curriculum relationships. Enforce roles, version checks, and independent approval in the future API; the prototype role selector is an experience demonstration.

Keep scenarios separate

Explore changes on a scratch copy. Carry rationale and affected relationships into review, then publish only after independent approval with an audit record.

Basis: Clio PRD, AI Engineering Brief v2, Complete Application Guide, and evaluation reports. Existing report findings below concern the prototype; production AI evaluation results and savings remain unmeasured.

Proposed data & service design

Preserve the decision.

Curriculum model

Program → catalog version → course → weekly outcome → competency alignment. Store the Introduced, Reinforced, or Mastered stage on the alignment.

Evidence model

Course → learning artifact → source reference → last-confirmed timestamp. Associate exports with an approved curriculum version.

Change model

Change request → affected relationships → rationale → reviewer decision → publication → audit event.

MVP & unresolved decisions

One reliable map first.
Connected intelligence next.

Smallest connected release

Persist a validated course map, enforce server-side roles and independent approval, and generate one versioned evidence package.

Integration gate

Connect and reconcile the catalog and Canvas before describing artifact freshness as verified.

Policy gate

The Complete Application Guide explains the chosen export exception: editors and approvers must resolve stale items; dean and accreditation reviewers may export for review. A useful production safeguard is to show freshness and snapshot status on those reviewer exports.

Data gate

The PRD varies between seven and eight mapped courses out of 21. Reconcile the coverage denominator and canonical catalog before publishing readiness metrics.

State-level reporting, item-level assessment tagging, learner-performance analytics, and multi-program expansion remain later scope. The adaptive tutoring uploads describe a separate learning experience; they are not evidence that Clio already integrates with tutoring.
